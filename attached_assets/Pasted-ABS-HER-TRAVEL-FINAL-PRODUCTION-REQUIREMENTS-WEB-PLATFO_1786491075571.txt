==================================================

8. PROFILE COMPLETION IS NOT VISA ELIGIBILITY
==================================================

THIS IS A CRITICAL BUSINESS RULE.

A profile being 100% complete DOES NOT mean the customer is eligible to apply for every visa.

Profile completion means:

- Account exists
- Customer is logged in
- Personal information is complete
- Passport is available
- Personal photo is available
- Required profile information is available

Visa eligibility is a SEPARATE check.

Visa eligibility is determined by the rules configured by ADMIN for the SPECIFIC VISA.

The customer must pass BOTH:

1. Profile Completion
2. Visa Eligibility

==================================================

When the customer clicks:

"Apply Now"

the system MUST check the customer's nationality against the rules configured by Admin for that specific visa.

If the nationality is not eligible:

BLOCK THE APPLICATION.

- Customer is logged in
- Profile is 100% complete
- Passport is valid
- Customer has GCC residence
- Customer has European residence
- Customer has a Schengen visa
